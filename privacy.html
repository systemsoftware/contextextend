Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

Context Extend saves text on your device and can sync saved items through Apple’s iCloud service when iCloud is available and enabled for the app. The app does not require a Context Extend account or send your saved content to the developer.

Information you add

When you add an item, Context Extend stores its name, description, and text content on your device. You can type the text or select a file. For selected PDF and readable text files, the app extracts text on your device and saves that extracted text as the item’s content. It does not retain the original selected file as an attachment. If iCloud sync is active, the saved name, description, and extracted or typed text can also be stored in your private iCloud database and synced to other devices using the same Apple Account.

iCloud syncing

When iCloud sync is active, Apple’s CloudKit service handles syncing. Sync depends on your Apple Account, iCloud settings, network access, and service availability. Changes may take time to appear on other devices. Apple provides and processes the iCloud service under its own privacy policy. Context Extend does not use a developer-operated sync server.

How your content is used

The app uses saved content to display your items and search them within the app. It also places item names, descriptions, and text in the device’s Spotlight index so Apple system features, including Spotlight, Siri, and Shortcuts, can find or open them. Those features are governed by your device settings and Apple’s privacy practices. Consider this before saving sensitive text.

Collection and sharing

Context Extend does not use an app account, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or tracking technology. It does not transmit your saved items to the developer or to a developer-operated server, and it does not sell your information. When iCloud sync is active, saved item data is sent to Apple’s iCloud service. Files you choose from another provider, device backups, and Apple system features may be handled by those services under their own terms and settings.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Saved items remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app. When iCloud sync is active, they may also remain in iCloud and on your other synced devices. Deleting an item in Context Extend removes its local copy, requests removal of its Spotlight entry, and syncs the deletion to iCloud and other devices when syncing is available; this may take time. Removing the app from one device removes its local app data under normal device behavior, but does not by itself delete copies in iCloud or on other devices. Copies in device backups or services you used to supply a file may remain according to your settings and those providers’ policies.

Your choices

  • Choose what text or files to add; the app does not read files until you select one.
  • Delete saved items in the app when you no longer want them stored, synced, or indexed. Allow time for deletions to sync before removing the app or turning off iCloud.
  • Manage the app’s iCloud access, Siri, Spotlight, and backup settings in your device’s system settings. Turning off iCloud for the app stops future syncing on that device; it does not necessarily remove data already stored in iCloud.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this page will be updated with a new effective date. Material changes will be described here before or when they take effect.
